README

This is a mod that changes Quake 4 into a roguelike game. You must fight waves of enemies to earn money, and you can use the money to buy weapons and powerups. Bosses spawn every 5 waves, and enemy HP increases as the waves go on. 

There are 3 base weapons, and each base weapon can be fused into another base weapon to make an advanced weapon. Each weapon has unique properties depending on their element. Fire-based weapons are focused on AoE damage, water-based weapons are focused on single-target damage, and earth-based weapons are shorter range and/or give you armor. Weapons can be placed in the primary or secondary slots. The weapon in the primary slot must be fired normally, but it is much stronger than normal. Weapons in the secondary slot deal half damage, and they are missing properties that they would have in the primary slot. In exchange, the secondary weapons fire automatically towards where you are looking. 

The mod also features 2 cheat commands: wave_killall (to immediately finish the current wave) and wave_givecash (gives 50000 cash).
